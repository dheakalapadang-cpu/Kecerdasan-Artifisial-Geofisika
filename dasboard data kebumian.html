💡 Executive Insights & Strategic Recommendations

1. Growth Pace Warning (2025)

2024 sales ($900.6K) grew ~42% over 2023. However, 2025 partial-year volume indicates a potential slowdown. Action: Review current pipeline and sales seasonality.

2. Critical Return Rate (~50%)

Nearly 1 in 2 orders are returned. This presents a major operational bottleneck and profit leakage. Action: Initiate quality control and logistics review immediately.

3. Revenue vs. Margin Leader

Electronics generates highest revenue (~$660K), but Furniture leads in margin (~33.5%). Dell (~32.9%) outperforms Apple on margin. Action: Shift promo focus to higher-margin lines.

4. Discount Erosion (-0.46 Corr)

Heavy discounting negatively correlates with profit margins. Discounts above 15% erode profitability significantly. Action: Enforce strict discount approval floors.

5. Regional Upside

West region leads in total sales and profit. North region has the lowest volume but highest margin efficiency (~32.3%). Action: Scale marketing in the North region.

6. Sales Rep Dispersion

Top performers like Prakash (~$91.5K) significantly outperform average reps. Action: Implement peer coaching and sales standardisation programs.
